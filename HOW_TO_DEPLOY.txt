DEPLOY THE MAZE SOLVER (pick ONE way)

WAY 1 - Easiest, no account setup (Netlify Drop)
  1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag this whole "maze-showcase" folder onto the page.
  3. You get a live link in seconds.

WAY 2 - Vercel
  1. Install Node.js from https://nodejs.org (one time).
  2. Mac: double-click deploy.command   (first time: right-click > Open)
     Windows: double-click deploy.bat
  3. Log in when asked, press Enter for every question,
     and copy the "Production" link at the end.

IMPORTANT: index.html must be directly inside the folder you deploy.
Do not deploy the folder that contains this folder.

Test first: double-click index.html to open it in your browser.
